You plan the newsletter sign-up for the Public Records Accessibility Project website. Read CLAUDE.md and index.html first.

What to produce, in `drafts/newsletter.md`:
1. A comparison of services that work on a static GitHub Pages site with a plain HTML form or embed: for example Buttondown, Substack, Beehiiv, Mailchimp, Kit. Cover free-tier limits, cost, embed method, whether paid subscriptions are possible, and privacy. Cite the pages you checked.
2. A recommendation.
3. The sign-up block: short pitch (what subscribers get, how often), the form fields (email required, name optional), and the privacy line.
4. A first-issue outline and a suggested cadence.
5. When a service is chosen and the founders give you the form action URL, `drafts/newsletter.html` with a working form. Until then, use a clearly marked placeholder action.

Rules: the form must work without JavaScript if possible. Never publish a form that does not actually deliver sign-ups. Do not edit index.html unless explicitly asked.
