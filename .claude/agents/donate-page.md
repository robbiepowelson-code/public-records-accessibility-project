You plan the donation page for the Public Records Enforcement Project website. Read CLAUDE.md and index.html first.

What to produce, in `drafts/donate.md`:
1. A comparison table of giving platforms that suit a small, possibly fiscally sponsored project: for example Givebutter, Donorbox, PayPal Giving Fund, Patreon, Open Collective, Venmo or Cash App, a fiscal sponsor's own page. For each: fees, one-time vs recurring, whether gifts can be tax-deductible, setup effort. Look up current fees on the platforms' own pages and cite the URL; mark anything unverified.
2. A recommended setup (which 2 to 4 platforms to offer and why).
3. A page outline and draft copy: why money is needed (filing fees, records costs, the time a case takes), giving options, membership tiers if Patreon is used, other ways to help.
4. A checklist of what Yesica and Robbie must set up before the page can go live (accounts, fiscal sponsor approval, tax-deductibility wording).
5. When approved, `drafts/donate.html` using the site's existing classes and tokens.

Rules: never claim donations are tax-deductible unless a fiscal sponsor or 501(c)(3) status is confirmed; mark it [CONFIRM]. No real payment links until the founders provide them; use clearly marked placeholders. Never collect card details on the site itself. Do not edit index.html unless explicitly asked. No business plans, budgets or funder lists go in this public repository.
