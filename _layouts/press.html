###

The Public Records Enforcement Project is run by Yesica Prado and Robbie Powelson. It requests California police use-of-force records, sues agencies that withhold them, and publishes what it obtains. It is not a law firm. Neither founder is a lawyer, and the project does not provide legal advice or legal representation.
